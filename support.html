Support

The tutor won't start

On first launch the app downloads its language model once (a few gigabytes) over Wi-Fi. Keep the app open until it finishes; after that everything works offline.

The microphone does nothing

Allow the microphone in Settings → ZARIVO, then hold the microphone button while you speak and let go to send.

Levels and exams

A level opens when you pass the exam of the level below it: reading, listening, writing and speaking, 25 points each; you need 60 points in all and at least 10 in every skill. Writing and speaking are marked by the AI tutor on your iPhone. The exam is modelled on certified exams but is not an official certificate.

Subscription

Level A1 is free. ZARIVO Premium opens levels A2–C1. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open the Premium screen and tap Restore. To cancel, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
