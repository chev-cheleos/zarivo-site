Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

ZARIVO: Language Tutor is an iPhone app published by Albatros Solutions ("we") that teaches Slovak. This policy explains what the app stores and what leaves your iPhone. The short version: the AI tutor, speech recognition and the voice all run on the device; your progress stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We never receive any of it.

We collect nothing

ZARIVO has no user accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code. We, and any third party, have no access to your data.

The AI tutor and your voice

Lili, the tutor, is a language model that runs entirely on your iPhone. What you write or say to her is processed on the device and never sent anywhere. When you hold the microphone, the app records only while you hold it and turns your speech into text on the device (Whisper); the recording is not saved and is never uploaded. iOS asks for microphone permission first; without it the rest of the app keeps working.

What the app stores

  • your learning progress — lessons, exercise and exam results, study time, streak, achievements;
  • your review deck, the words you add, and the mistakes notebook;
  • conversations with the tutor and your homework texts;
  • your settings, name and an optional profile picture you choose (stored only on this iPhone).

This is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, progress, words, conversations and homework also sync through Apple's CloudKit private database in your own iCloud account, so they survive a new phone. That database belongs to you; we cannot read it. Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy.

Internet connections the app makes

  • The tutor model download: once, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public file host. Like any download, the host sees your IP address; nothing about you or your learning is sent.
  • Radio and podcasts: only when you open them, streams and feeds are loaded from the broadcaster (STVR: icecast.stv.livebox.sk, api.stvr.sk) and the podcast hosts (Buzzsprout, Anchor/Spotify).

Lessons, exercises, exams, texts, the dictionary and the voices are inside the app and work offline.

Subscription

Level A1 is free. ZARIVO Premium (levels A2–C1) is an auto-renewable subscription bought through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details, and the app learns from iOS only whether a subscription is active. You can cancel at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules it locally on the device. No push server is involved.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

All your data is on your device and in your own iCloud: you can see it in the app and delete it by deleting the app and, for iCloud, in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. We hold no copy, so there is nothing we could access, change or disclose.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app stores or adds any service that leaves the device, this page will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date.
